What awaits you

I

Swipe combat

Strike with a swipe. An orange bar means block, a red one means dodge. Perfect timing slows time and doubles your next hit.

II

Classes and paths

Warrior, Mage or Gatherer. At class level 15 choose one of two paths, and your hero's look changes with every step.

III

400+ items

Weapons and armor with levels, rarities from common to mythic, and sets whose bonuses change how you fight.

IV

Torin's forge

Upgrade gear up to +10, fuse three items into a rarer one and repair worn armor before the next fight.

V

Dungeons and artifacts

Daily dungeons for gold and XP, and a fog-covered artifact dungeon where every step is a choice.

VI

A living city

Story quests, guild contracts, secret quests and characters with their own pasts. Not everyone is who they seem.

The world of Erashal

A thousand years ago five heroes sealed Malgarot, the Demon King. None of them came home. Now the seal is cracking, the forests are full of shadows, and this world no longer gives birth to heroes of its own.

So a god searched beyond it, and found you. Ashal, the last free city, is waiting.

"Thousands of souls. Almost all of them went out the moment I showed them what lay ahead. Yours is still burning."

FAQ

Is the game free?

Yes. Dark Throne is free to download and play.

Which platforms?

Android now (closed test on Google Play). iOS is planned next.

Do I need internet?

You can play offline. Sign in with Google Play Games to keep your progress in the cloud and restore it on a new phone.

Which languages?

English, Russian and Ukrainian. You can switch the language in the settings.

Who makes it?

Downpour Studio, a solo indie developer. Follow the devlogs on YouTube and TikTok.

I found a bug or lost progress

Write to darkthronerpg@gmail.com and include your Player ID (Hero profile → Copy).
