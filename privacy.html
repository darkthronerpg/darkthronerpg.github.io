Privacy Policy — Dark Throne: Isekai RPG

Effective date: November 21, 2026 (version 0.1.3 and later) Developer: Downpour Studio Contact: darkthronerpg@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Dark Throne: Isekai RPG ("the Game") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect

The Game never asks for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or payment details.

Game account. When you start the Game, an anonymous account is created for you automatically (Firebase Authentication). It has a random Player ID and no password. You can see your Player ID in the Game (Profile / Account). If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your anonymous account is linked to your Play Games profile. We receive your Play Games player ID and display name. We do not receive your email address or password.

Cloud save. To keep your progress safe and restore it on a new device, your game progress is saved to our cloud database (Cloud Firestore): your characters and the names you gave them, levels, items, currency, quests and other in-game progress, together with the device model, app version and the time of the save. Device settings (sound, graphics, language) are not uploaded.

Time verification. To keep daily rewards and timers fair, the Game regularly requests the current time from our server.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): - App interactions — for example, screens opened, battles started and finished, levels reached, quests completed. - Identifiers — an app-instance ID, the Android Advertising ID and your Player ID. - Approximate location — a general region (country or city) derived from a masked IP address. Your precise location is never collected. - Technical information — device model, operating system version, app version, language.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the Game crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with the technical details of the problem (stack trace, device model, OS version, the state of the Game and recent in-game events), an installation ID and your Player ID.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config): to receive settings such as maintenance notices or in-game events, the Game sends your country, language, time zone and app version.

2. How we use this information

  • to save your progress and restore it on another device or after reinstalling;
  • to let you sign in with Google Play Games;
  • to keep the Game fair (time checks, protection of saves against tampering);
  • to find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • to understand how the Game is played and improve balance and content;
  • to show maintenance notices, update requirements and in-game events.

We do not use this information to identify you as a person, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The services below process data on our behalf as service providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services (only if you sign in) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your cloud save is stored on Google Cloud servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Google may process some data in other countries in line with its privacy policy.

4. Advertising

The Game currently contains no advertising.

5. Where your progress is stored

Your progress is stored on your device and in the cloud. - Signed in with Google Play Games: your progress is linked to your Play Games profile and can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling. - Playing anonymously: your progress is linked to this installation of the Game. If you uninstall the Game or change your phone without signing in, it may not be possible to restore it.

6. Data retention

  • Analytics data is automatically deleted after 2 months.
  • Crash reports are automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Your account and cloud save are kept for as long as you use the Game, so that your progress is not lost. If you ask us to delete them, we do so as described below.

7. Deleting your account and data

To delete your game account and all data linked to it (cloud save, Player ID, analytics and crash reports linked to your Player ID), email darkthronerpg@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and include your Player ID (in the Game: Profile / Account). We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your Android device settings.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules on our database allow each account to read and write only its own save. Saves are signed to detect tampering.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. To use these rights, contact us at darkthronerpg@gmail.com with your Player ID.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when new features are added to the Game (for example, in-game mail or support requests). Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at darkthronerpg@gmail.com.
